1. Executive Summary

EduPulse is an enterprise-grade academic management system designed to streamline institutional governance, faculty workflows, and student success tracking. Built on a clean, decoupled architecture, it offers continuous attendance compliance tracking, automated rubric grading, multi-factor risk diagnostics, interactive GPA simulation, and official academic audit transcripts.

3. Academic Intelligence Mathematical Formulation

The platform computes real-time academic composite scores using weighted multi-factor diagnostic equations:

Composite Score = (0.40 × Exam Average) + (0.35 × Assignment Average) + (0.25 × Attendance Rate)
⚡ Compliance & Risk Heuristics
• Low Risk (≥75%): Good academic standing.
• Medium Risk (60% - 74%): Subject gaps flagged for remediation.
• High Risk (<60% or Attendance <75%): Automatic counselor alert dispatch.
📊 Grade Calculation Engine
• Standard 4.0 GPA calculation algorithm.

• "What-If" GPA recovery projections.
